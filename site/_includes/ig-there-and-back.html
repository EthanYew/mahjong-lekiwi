Why practice hid the error

The camera and the arm disagreed about where things were by 4 to 31 mm, depending on where on the table.

Practice: back to the same spot

The error was the same on the way out and back, so it cancelled itself out. Setting tiles down looked solved.

A real game: to a different spot

Tiles travelled further, the errors at A and B no longer matched, and carried tiles landed 15–19 mm off target, always in the same direction.
