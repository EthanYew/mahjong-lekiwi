Three kinds of work

Each step in a turn is done by one of these.

Geometry

Inverse kinematics

The maths that works out how each motor needs to turn to place the gripper (robot claw) at a specific spot in space.

Same answer every time

Like working out how to move your shoulder and elbow to reach a point with your fingers.

Rules

Ordinary scripted code

Deterministic and predictable output.

PredictableCheap to buildRigid

A tile tilted at an odd angle: a scripted pickup is more likely to struggle.

Learned

A neural network trained on examples

Needs lots of time and effort to collect enough data and train the policy itself.

AdaptableNot deterministicExpensive

A tile tilted at an odd angle: handled better, if it was trained on similar examples.
