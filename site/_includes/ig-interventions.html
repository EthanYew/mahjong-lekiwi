What counts against the 3

A clean hand allows a person no more than 3 interventions, and the audit at the end must find zero mismatches.

Counts as an intervention

Assist

I correct a misread tile label, or physically reposition a tile, so the game can continue.

Safety stop

I press Esc to halt the arm and prevent physical damage.

Reset

I reset the arm after a tile lands more than 25 mm from where it should.

Doesn’t count

Designed step

Something I am meant to do, such as setting up the wall each turn.

Confirmation ask

I approve a tile read without changing it. Not an intervention, but I count them, because each one is a person watching and waiting.

Recovery

The robot notices its own failure and retries.
