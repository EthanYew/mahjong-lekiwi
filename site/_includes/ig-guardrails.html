Three safeguards between a bug and the arm

Against erratic, sudden movements, especially from a learned model.

A command or a policy output

1
Permission first

Any command my AI coding agents run that would move the arm stops and asks me. I sit beside the arm and can stop it any time.

2
Always approach from above, always rise first

Joint speed and movement range are capped. I added this after the arm once shot straight to its zero position, because its starting pose in training differed from where I ran the policy.

3
Hard limits and fail-safes
  • Any step that would put the gripper tips more than 5 mm below the table is scaled back.
  • Impossible detections are discarded.
  • A camera fault stops the pickup.
  • When I’m watching, the robot asks me about tile names it’s unsure of rather than guessing.
