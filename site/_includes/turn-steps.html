One turn · six steps · three kinds of work

Every turn, the robot runs this loop

  1. STEP 1
    Learned

    Find

    YOLO on the overhead camera spots each tile; depth turns its pixels into a 3D position. Face-down tiles in hand are tracked by saved slots.

  2. STEP 2
    Geometry

    Get above

    Inverse kinematics moves the hand over the tile, and a correction learned from hundreds of measured hovers cleans up the error.

  3. STEP 3
    Learned

    Grasp

    An ACT policy lowers the hand, grabs the tile and lifts it, watching through the wrist camera.

  4. STEP 4
    Learned

    Read

    The arm shows a drawn tile to the camera on its wheeled base, and a small classifier names it.

  5. STEP 5
    Rules

    Decide

    Game code picks the discard that brings the hand closest to winning.

  6. STEP 6
    GeometryLearned

    Return

    The drawn tile fills the discard’s gap, aimed by a correction fitted from over 1,000 measured set-downs.
